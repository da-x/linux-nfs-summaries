Case Folding v7: Review Feedback on VFS Initialization and ext4 Casefold Reporting

Case Folding v7: Review Feedback on VFS Initialization and ext4 Casefold Reporting

Type: New feature / protocol enhancement (continued from prior week)

Prior Week Recap

Chuck Lever posted version 7 of his 16-patch series "Exposing case folding behavior" on January 22nd. The sole change since v6 was the removal of a memset from vfs_fileattr_get() to fix an initialization bug flagged by Jan Kara, where relocated struct initialization was inadvertently zeroing out the flags_valid hint before filesystem callbacks could read it. See last week's summary for full context on the series, which introduces a kernel-wide fileattr_get mechanism enabling filesystems to report case sensitivity and case preservation characteristics — critical for NFSD, ksmbd, and NFS client interoperability.

This Week's Activity: Review Feedback on V7

Five messages were exchanged on January 23rd, comprising review feedback from Darrick J. Wong (XFS maintainer) and Jan Kara (ext4/VFS maintainer), along with responses from Chuck Lever. The discussion focused on two patches: the foundational VFS infrastructure patch (01/16) and the ext4 implementation (07/16).

1. VFS Infrastructure Patch (01/16): Reviewed-by from Darrick, Minor Fix Requested by Jan Kara

Darrick J. Wong provided a positive review of patch 01/16 [id://20260123002646.GL5945@frogsfrogsfrogs], confirming the UAPI changes and the corrected initialization scheme:

"The UAPI changes still look ok to me. AFAICT the file_kattr initialization now seem like they don't zap fields to confuse vfs_fileattr_get."

He granted his Reviewed-by tag, though he noted a minor organizational concern:

"In hindsight I regret not asking for the file_kattr initialization change to be in a separate patch."

Chuck Lever responded [id://bb2bb55c-6078-4494-9851-c684dfd9aa8c@app.fastmail.com], acknowledging this feedback:

"If I'm asked to post another revision of this series, I'll look at splitting 1/16 into two patches."

Jan Kara also weighed in on patch 01/16 [id://3drnol5zeenodg22c26yswnsk3pzn4csnwdbkgmicfmk4rrpkk@neidpou4eqgb], expressing overall approval but identifying a remaining initialization gap:

"This scheme looks good. But AFAICT declared 'fa' needs to be zeroed-out also in file_getattr()? Otherwise the patch looks good to me."

This points to a potential bug where the struct file_kattr variable fa in file_getattr() may not be properly zero-initialized, which could lead to stale or uninitialized data being passed through the VFS layer. This is a notable concern for NFS client development since file_getattr() is one of the primary entry points through which case sensitivity information flows from filesystems to userspace and server-side consumers like NFSD.

2. ext4 Casefold Reporting (07/16): Question About VFS-Level Casefold Handling

Darrick J. Wong raised an architectural question about the ext4 implementation [id://20260123002904.GM5945@frogsfrogsfrogs], questioning whether casefold detection should be handled at the VFS level rather than in individual filesystem implementations:

"Curious. Shouldn't the VFS set FS_XFLAG_CASEFOLD if the VFS casefolding flag is set?"

He noted a seeming inconsistency:

"OTOH, there are more filesystems that apparently support casefolding (given the size of this patchset) than actually set S_CASEFOLD. I think I'm ignorant of something here..."

This raises an important design question about whether the FS_XFLAG_CASEFOLD flag should be derived automatically from the VFS-level S_CASEFOLD inode flag, rather than requiring each filesystem to explicitly set it in its fileattr_get callback. If many filesystems support casefolding but don't set S_CASEFOLD, there may be a mismatch between what the VFS layer knows and what individual filesystem implementations report.

Chuck Lever responded [id://95aa2a65-e1e8-44ef-a62a-e3190a11cb32@app.fastmail.com], indicating uncertainty about whether any review action was required on this point:

"I'm not clear if there's a review action needed. Help?"

This suggests the question remains open and may need clarification from VFS or ext4 maintainers in future rounds.

Significance for NFS Client Development

The feedback this week touches on two areas directly relevant to NFS client correctness:

  1. Initialization completeness: Jan Kara's observation about file_getattr() needing zero-initialization is important because the NFS client's fileattr_get implementation (patch 10/16 of the series) depends on clean struct initialization to correctly report case sensitivity information retrieved from servers via PATHCONF (NFSv3) or FATTR4 attributes (NFSv4). Uninitialized fields could cause NFSD to misreport the case behavior of re-exported NFS mounts.

  2. VFS vs. filesystem-level casefold reporting: Darrick's question about whether FS_XFLAG_CASEFOLD should be set automatically by the VFS when S_CASEFOLD is present has implications for how reliably NFSD can discover case sensitivity across diverse underlying filesystems — including NFS mounts themselves, where the client must accurately propagate server-reported case semantics.

Status and Outlook

The v7 series has received positive review signals: Darrick J. Wong granted his Reviewed-by on patch 01/16, and Jan Kara indicated the approach "looks good" pending a minor fix to file_getattr(). Two items remain for potential future revisions:

  • Patch splitting: The initialization change in patch 01/16 may be split into a separate patch per Darrick's suggestion.
  • file_getattr() zero-initialization: Jan Kara's identified gap needs to be addressed.
  • VFS-level casefold flag question: Darrick's architectural question about S_CASEFOLD vs. FS_XFLAG_CASEFOLD remains unresolved and may warrant discussion with VFS maintainers.

The series continues to advance toward eventual merge through the VFS tree, targeting kernel version v6.20 or later.
