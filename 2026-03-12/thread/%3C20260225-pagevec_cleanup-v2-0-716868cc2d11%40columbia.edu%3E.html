Final Reviews: pagevec Cleanup Series Completes Review Cycle

Summary

Final Reviews for pagevec Cleanup Series

Summary

This thread represents the tail end of a four-patch series by Tal Zussman to clean up residual references to struct pagevec following its removal from the kernel. Last week, the series was introduced and received initial reviews from Andrew Morton, Jan Kara, David Hildenbrand, Zi Yan, and Chris Li. This week, Lorenzo Stoakes (Oracle) provided final Reviewed-by tags for all four patches, completing the review cycle.

Discussion Evolution

The series, originally posted last week, aimed to modernize headers and macros after struct pagevec was removed in commit 1e0877d58b1e. The changes involved:

  1. Removing stray forward declarations of struct pagevec.
  2. Removing unnecessary #include <linux/pagevec.h> directives from .c files.
  3. Renaming include/linux/pagevec.h to include/linux/folio_batch.h.
  4. Renaming the macro PAGEVEC_SIZE to FOLIO_BATCH_SIZE.

This week, Lorenzo Stoakes provided his reviews for each patch:

NFS Client Impact

The impact on NFS client development remains minimal and cosmetic. As noted in last week's summary, Patch 2/4 removes unused header includes from two NFS files (fs/nfs/blocklayout/blocklayout.c and fs/nfs/dir.c). These changes do not alter NFS protocol handling, performance, or introduce any bug fixes; they simply reduce header dependency clutter in the NFS codebase.

Conclusion

This thread marks the completion of reviews for a successful infrastructure cleanup series. With Lorenzo Stoakes' final Reviewed-by tags, the series has gathered sufficient maintainer approval for integration. The changes represent routine maintenance with no functional impact on NFS client behavior or protocol handling.
