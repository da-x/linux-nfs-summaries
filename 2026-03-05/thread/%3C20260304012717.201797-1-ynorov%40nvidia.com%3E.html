Rejection of rest_of_page() macro globalisation affecting NFS

Summary

Overview

This week saw a brief but contentious discussion regarding a patch series by Yury Norov aimed at globalizing the rest_of_page() macro. The series, titled "[PATCH 0/8] mm: globalize rest_of_page() macro," sought to move a helper function from the net/9p driver into core/mm and apply it tree-wide across various subsystems, including the NFS client.

The discussion was characterized by immediate and strong opposition from several kernel maintainers, who argued that the macro obscured simple arithmetic rather than clarifying it. The series appears to have been rejected in its current form.

Technical Arguments and Evolution

The Proposal

Yury Norov introduced an eight-patch series to replace instances of PAGE_SIZE - offset_in_page(ptr) with a new rest_of_page(ptr) macro.

"The net/9p networking driver has a handy macro to calculate the amount of bytes from a given pointer to the end of page. Move it to core/mm, and apply tree-wide. No functional changes intended." Yury Norov

NFS Client Impact: Patch 2/8 specifically targeted the filesystem layer, including a modification to fs/nfs/pagelist.c. This change was purely cosmetic, intended to align NFS code with the proposed global standard without altering functionality.

The Backlash

The proposal faced swift criticism from maintainers across networking, storage, and virtualization subsystems.

Jakub Kicinski questioned the utility of the macro, arguing that it adds cognitive load for developers who must now learn a new helper to perform "obvious and easy to comprehend math." Jakub Kicinski

Jens Axboe, the I/O subsystem maintainer, echoed this sentiment, emphasizing readability:

"The thing is, now I have to go lookup what on earth rest_of_page() does, whereas PAGE_SIZE - offset_in_page(page) is immediately obvious. It's a classic case of 'oh let's add this helper to simplify things' which really just makes it worse... IOW, just no." Jens Axboe

Sean Christopherson from the KVM team and Peter Zijlstra also joined the opposition. Peter Zijlstra expressed a broader disdain for such abstractions:

"I hate these pointless helpers that obscure perfectly sensible and obvious code -- and for me that includes things like rounddown() and DIV_ROUND_UP(). It just makes the code harder to read." Peter Zijlstra

Author's Defense

Yury Norov attempted to justify the change by highlighting cases where the arithmetic becomes bulky, particularly in complex conditional statements. He argued that the macro reduces the mental effort required to decode such lines:

"My motivation is that it helps to simplify constructions... To a point where I don't have to use my brains to decode them. I agree it's an easy math. It's just too bulky to my (and 9p guys too) taste." Yury Norov

Final Conclusions

The discussion concluded with a clear consensus against the introduction of the rest_of_page() macro. The maintainers' preference for explicit, self-documenting arithmetic over abstracted helpers for simple page calculations suggests that the NFS client code in fs/nfs/pagelist.c will likely retain its current implementation or revert if any changes were applied. No further iterations or acceptance were observed within this week's window.
