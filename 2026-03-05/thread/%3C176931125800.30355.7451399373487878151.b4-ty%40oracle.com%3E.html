Summary

nfsd: Cred Refcount Leak Fix — Patch Status Confirmed for 7.0-rc

Classification

Bug fix. This thread concerns a credential reference-count leak in the NFS server (nfsd) netlink handler code, originally reported and patched by Kuniyuki Iwashima in January 2026.

Background (Prior Weeks)

In late January 2026, Kuniyuki Iwashima submitted a two-patch series fixing credential reference-count leaks in nfsd_nl_listener_set_doit() and nfsd_nl_threads_set_doit(). The root cause was misuse of get_current_cred(), which increments the credential reference count but was not being properly balanced with a corresponding release. Chuck Lever applied both patches to his nfsd-testing tree, citing commits c14b0c3b5966 and 687b9b69fcda as confirmed in the thread root.

This Week's Activity

This week saw a brief exchange between Kuniyuki Iwashima and Chuck Lever to confirm the upstream status of these fixes. While rebasing his local branch, Kuniyuki noticed that the two patches were not yet visible in mainline or easily locatable in Chuck's public tree:

"While rebasing my local branch, I just noticed both patches are not in the mainline and I couldn't find both SHA1 in your tree. Could you double check?" Kuniyuki Iwashima

Chuck Lever clarified that the patches reside in his nfsd-fixes branch and are slated for imminent submission:

"The patches are in my nfsd-fixes branch. I'm planning to submit them soon for 7.0-rc." Chuck Lever

Kuniyuki acknowledged the update:

"Sounds good, sorry for bothering you, and thank you for checking!" Kuniyuki Iwashima

Key Technical Points

  • The bug: get_current_cred() was called in netlink handler functions without a matching put_cred(), leading to a credential reference-count leak. Over time, this could exhaust credential references and potentially cause memory pressure or denial-of-service conditions on heavily loaded NFS servers.
  • Affected code paths: nfsd_nl_listener_set_doit() and nfsd_nl_threads_set_doit() — both are netlink-based administration interfaces for configuring NFSD listener sockets and thread counts.
  • Impact scope: This is an nfsd (server-side) fix, not an NFS client change. However, it is relevant to overall NFS subsystem stability, as credential leaks in the server can degrade performance under sustained administrative operations or high-frequency netlink reconfigurations.

Conclusion

The cred refcount leak patches remain pending in Chuck Lever's nfsd-fixes branch, with a planned submission window targeting the Linux 7.0-rc cycle. No further technical debate occurred this week; the exchange served to confirm that the fixes are queued and on track for mainline inclusion.
