RDMA ib_device_attr int→u32: author commits to v7 rework of min_t() pattern

Summary

RDMA: ib_device_attr capability fields int→u32 — Vennela commits to a v7 rework

This week in one line

The thread moved from objection to commitment: Erni Sri Satya Vennela (Microsoft) replied on 2026-06-06 to Andy Shevchenko's min_t() pushback from the prior week, confirming the series will be redone as v7 (skipping a v6, since the v5 was mis-targeted at net-next rather than rdma-next).

"Thankyou for the suggestion, Andy. I'll be updating this in the next version v7." — Vennela

That single message is the only in-window activity this week; no new patch revision has landed yet, and no other reviewer has weighed in during 2026-06-05 → 2026-06-11.

Bridge from last week (brief)

Last week's summary covered the v5 posting on 2026-06-01, Vennela's self-correction about the wrong tree prefix ("net-next" instead of "rdma-next"), and Shevchenko's pointed objection to the min_t() pattern in the rxe driver:

"Just no. min_t() usage has to be very well justified. It's a beast which may stub one in the back. As Linus said in most of the cases one wants clamp() rather than min(). Please, redo this and similar pieces." — Shevchenko

The series is a bug fix / type-correctness cleanup, not a new feature or protocol change: it widens the signed int capability counters in struct ib_device_attr to u32 (they are inherently non-negative, and drivers already cache them as u32), then ripples through every consumer — IB core, iSER/iSERt, SRP/SRPT, RTRS, iPOIB, NVMe-oF host/target, RDS, SMBdirect, and the NFS/RDMA stack (net/sunrpc/xprtrdma/verbs.c, frwr_ops.c, svc_rdma_transport.c, include/linux/sunrpc/svc_rdma.h).

Why this matters for NFS clients

The NFS/RDMA surface is what keeps this thread in front of linux-nfs readers. Two pieces of the diff are genuine correctness fixes rather than mechanical type churn:

  • frwr_ops.c u32 underflow guard — the bounds check is reordered to happen before a subtraction that would otherwise underflow once the operands are unsigned. This is a real NFS-over-RDMA client bug fix and drew no reviewer objection last week, so it should survive into v7 largely intact.
  • min_t(int, …) → min_t(u32, …) in svc_rdma_transport.c — part of the series-wide signedness cleanup, but this specific hunk is exactly the kind Shevchenko asked to be redone with clamp() or an explicit self-documenting bounds check.

The rest of the NFS/RDMA changes (verbs.c, svc_rdma.h) are mechanical consumer updates that follow from the field widening.

State of play / what to watch next

  • Classification: bug fix (signedness / type-boundary), with a large mechanical consumer update across RDMA and NFS/RDMA.
  • Open items for v7: (1) re-target to rdma-next, (2) replace the min_t() pattern with clamp() or equivalent per Shevchenko's request — "redo this and similar pieces" was series-wide, not rxe-only, so the NFS/RDMA hunks are in scope too, (3) keep the U8_MAX clamps and the frwr_ops.c underflow guard, which drew no objection.
  • No v6 exists. Vennela's reply explicitly jumps to "v7," consistent with last week's note that a re-targeted revision was already needed regardless of feedback.
  • Next expected event: a v7 posting on rdma-next (which will also land on linux-nfs because of the NFS/RDMA hunks). Until then, the thread is in a quiet waiting state — one author acknowledgment, no further review.
