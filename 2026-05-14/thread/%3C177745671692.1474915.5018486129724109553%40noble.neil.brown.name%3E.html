NFSv4: mkdir retry -EEXIST fix — maintainer follow-up, no response yet

Summary

NFSv4: Follow-up on -EEXIST Regression Fix for mkdir(2) After Retry

Status This Week: Patch Inquiry, No Resolution Yet

This week saw a single message in this thread: a polite but pointed inquiry from Thorsten Leemhuis, who tracks kernel regressions, asking whether Igor Raits' regression-fix patch had "fallen through the cracks." As of the end of this window, no response from the NFSv4 maintainers was recorded, and the patch remains unmerged.

Background (Bridged from Prior Week)

Last week, Igor Raits submitted a surgical 5-line fix for a regression in the NFSv4 client where mkdir(2) could return spurious -EEXIST after a successful retry following an NFS4ERR_DELAY. The root cause was traced to commit dd862da61e91, which introduced a conditional gate around nfs4_handle_exception(), breaking the retry-state hygiene that other NFSv4 procedures (such as nfs4_proc_symlink() and nfs4_proc_link()) rely on. The patch was reviewed by NeilBrown and marked for stable backport.

This Week's Activity

On May 13, Thorsten Leemhuis followed up directly to the NFSv4 maintainers:

"@NFSv4 maintainers, just wondering, did this patch maybe fall through the cracks? It fixes a regression, that's why it's on my radar. Or was there some progress and I missed it?" Thorsten Leemhuis

This message underscores the importance of the fix: it addresses a confirmed regression that causes intermittent mkdir(2) failures under high-concurrency workloads — a real-world reliability issue for NFS clients. The patch is minimal (2 insertions, 3 deletions in fs/nfs/nfs4proc.c), well-tested (with a concrete reproducer provided), and carries a Reviewed-by from NeilBrown along with a Cc: stable@vger.kernel.org tag.

Classification

  • Type: Bug fix (regression repair)
  • Component: NFSv4 client (fs/nfs/nfs4proc.c)
  • Impact: Userspace receives spurious -EEXIST from mkdir(2) after successful retry, causing intermittent failures in concurrent directory-creation workloads.

Conclusion

The thread remains open with no maintainer response this week. The patch is ready for merge and backport, pending maintainer action. Leemhuis' follow-up highlights the ongoing need for timely attention to regression fixes in the NFS client subsystem.
