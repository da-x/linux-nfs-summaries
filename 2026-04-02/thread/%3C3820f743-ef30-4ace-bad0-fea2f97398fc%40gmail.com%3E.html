Background and Context

The original bug report from March 9 flagged a WARNING condition in nfs_free_request(), specifically related to a stale PG_WB_END page flag during NFS read completion when FS-Cache is enabled. The warning suggests a race or incorrect flag management between the NFS client's page cache operations and FS-Cache's writeback tracking mechanisms.

This issue is part of a broader pattern of Mike Owen's investigations into NFS/fscache interactions in production re-export deployments. Earlier this year, he resolved a separate months-long investigation into NFS re-export server lockups with fscache — which turned out to be an EXT4 tuning issue rather than a kernel bug (see prior summary).

Related Infrastructure Work

This week also saw significant activity in the netfslib/cachefiles subsystem — David Howells submitted a 26-patch series replacing folio_queue with segmented bio_vec[] chains (bvecq) here. While not directly addressing Owen's warning, this series includes embedded bug fixes relevant to NFS fscache operations:

  • A fix for read abandonment during retry (Patch 05/26)
  • A VM_BUG_ON_FOLIO race condition fix (Patch 03/26)
  • An excess dput() fix in cachefiles (Patch 07/26)

These changes strengthen the reliability of the infrastructure underlying NFS fscache operations, and may indirectly affect the conditions under which Owen's warning manifests.
