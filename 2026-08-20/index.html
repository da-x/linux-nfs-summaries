NFS Weekly Summaries

  1. Summaries

Client XDR holes, stale mkdir negatives, and pNFS recovery races dominate a dense NFS week

Week ending 2026-08-20

The Linux NFS list spent the week of 14–20 August 2026 on client correctness, not new wire protocol. Decoder wrap bugs, a 6.15 mkdir regression that leaves stale negatives hashed, pNFS layoutreturn races, and a pin-aware Direct I/O respin all landed in the same window. On the server side, Linus merged the NFSD v7.3 pull (CB_NOTIFY directory delegations) while lockd, rpcbind, and nfsd_mutex hangs kept the control plane busy.

Critical client bugs

Decoder and slot-table holes against a malicious server

Three independent authors hit the same u32 * 4 wrap in xdr_inline_decode() within 48 hours. Alibaba’s Chuyf26 posted word-at-a-time fixes for pNFS layout-type decode on FSINFO and the GETDEVICEINFO notification bitmap. Griffin Kroah-Hartman then offered a four-line pre-multiply guard at the same two sites. None of the three has an in-window ack. Trond Myklebust already told a June campaign to use xdr_stream_decode_uint32_array() rather than open-coding the bound; that is still the merge-shaped close.

Paula Moutafian added two more client memory-safety reports. Zero back-channel ca_maxrequests underflows the NFSv4.1 callback slot-id ceiling to 0xffffffff, then __set_bit() writes past the 1024-bit used_slots[] on a server-chosen CB_SEQUENCE slot. A two-line reject in nfs4_verify_back_channel_attrs() matches the existing fore-channel check. A day later she showed that xdr_inline_pages() wraps the tail length when AUTH_SYS re-encodes after a GARBAGE_ARGS reply raises au_ralign against a buffer sized from the old au_rslack. KASAN on v7.2-rc7 recorded a 1664-byte slab write on an ordinary NFSv3 TCP read(2). Both patches are unreviewed.

Jérémy Jean closed a long-standing CB_SEQUENCE empty referring-call kfree: kmalloc_objs() left rcl_refcalls uninitialized when rcl_nrefcalls was zero. Anna Schumaker asked for kzalloc_objs() at allocation time; v2 does that and still awaits an ack.

Stale negative after failed mkdir

Amazon’s Jimmy Zuber restored d_drop() on a failed nfs_mkdir(). Since 6.15’s “mkdir returns a dentry” conversion, an EEXIST (or any RPC error) leaves the original negative hashed. Applications that treat EEXIST as “the directory is there” then get spurious ENOENT on open/stat of that name or its children. Against knfsd/NFSv4.1 with nosharecache, 200/200 loser reads failed unpatched and 200/200 succeeded patched. Sibling nfs_create() / nfs_mknod() still drop. Unreviewed, Cc: stable for v6.15+.

This is not NeilBrown’s same-week negative-dentry RFC. Zuber’s bug is the wrong negative still hashed; Neil’s series is parent d_lockref overflow and soft lockups walking huge d_children lists.

lockd client: NULL deref and a leak on the same -ENOMEM path

Ran Hongyun’s one-liner for nlmclnt_locks_release_private was right on the crash (fl_ops installed even when lockowner allocation failed) but Jeff Layton asked him to inline the helper and also drop the nlmclnt_alloc_call() references. The two-patch rewrite does both. Layton Reviewed-by’d it and pinged Anna Schumaker / Trond Myklebust to pick it up as client-side lockd.

Separately, Shuangpeng Bai hit a KASAN UAF in nlm_async_call during nfsd unmount with a lock still blocked. Chuck Lever’s v1 pin of the head of nlm_blocked was necessary but not sufficient: sashiko showed the extra ref lets teardown-unlinked blocks get requeued. v2 keeps the pin and adds B_DEAD so nlmsvc_insert_block_locked() cannot resurrect a retired block. Server-side code, client-visible GRANT callbacks.

xprtrdma hang and pipefs UAF

Tim Menninger (Pure Storage) diagnosed an rpciod hang: frwr_unmap_sync() posts LOCAL_INV after rpcrdma_xprt_disconnect() has drained the QP, then waits forever. Chuck Lever agreed on the race, rejected a new inflight counter, and moved the Fixes: tag to the v5.7 freeable rx_ep. v2 serializes with an rwsem, reuses rpcrdma_req / endpoint refs, and survived 200 pNFS DS-restart cycles.

Shigeru Yoshida’s pipefs_sb notifier update closes a UAF when NFS clones exist before rpc_pipefs is mounted: clnt->pipefs_sb stayed NULL, pipedir teardown was skipped, leftover info files pointed at freed rpc_clnt. Jeff Layton Reviewed-by.

pNFS client: wide layouts, recovery storms, and a NACK

Hammerspace’s Benjamin Coddington had a productive and a paused series. Jeff Layton Reviewed-by’d the whole LAYOUTGET buffer-sizing set: treat NFS4ERR_TOOSMALL as layout-unavailable (stable fix), then grow the reply buffer to the session maximum so wide flexfiles layouts actually fetch. A 21-patch device-notification and cache-scaling series was paused the same day for sashiko findings; v2 is promised. Tigran Mkrtchyan Reviewed-by’d the independently useful DS-setup opnum so connection failures report READ/WRITE/COMMIT instead of OP_ILLEGAL.

Trond Myklebust NACK’d Menninger’s “don’t mark a replacement layout for return from a stale lseg” patch. The FlexFiles restart storm is real (94 of 96 CPUs, no recovery); gating layoutreturn on NFS_LSEG_VALID would drop the error report the server still needs. The intended collector is the existing EBUSY serialization.

That same invariant drove the EBUSY sign fix. Commit 41d0a8ead9720 compared against unsigned EBUSY, so busy segments never deferred LAYOUTRETURN. v1 over-corrected to == 0; Trond rejected that because it would skip recall replies on empty layouts (soft/softerr). v2 is the one-line -EBUSY check with -ENOENT preserved.

Junrui Luo’s unreviewed trio covers LAYOUTSTATS send-buffer exhaustion (lock held after xdr_reserve_space() NULL), DS-cache aliasing across NFS versions, and an lseg leak on pnfs_layout_process()’s forget path.

Client features still in flight

Pranjal Shrivastava posted DIO modernization v6 after Sashiko found double-unpins in v5’s pin-aware nfs_page path. PG_PINNED + wb_nr_pinned make GUP pins a first-class nfs_page property; Direct I/O moves to iov_iter_extract_pages() so a later series can pass ITER_ALLOW_P2PDMA. Hellwig’s Reviewed-by tags are restored. Folio aggregation and actual P2P enablement stay out of scope.

Matthew Wilcox’s drop aops->launder_folio would convert NFS invalidate onto bulk filemap_invalidate_pages() and delete nfs_launder_folio(). No NFS maintainer has replied; the only review is a FUSE range-semantics objection.

Manjunath Patil’s timeout printk adds client id, transport id, connection cookie, and task owner so a soft-mount stall can be matched to rpcctl. Petr Vorel Reviewed-by’d it.

Mariia Nikitash’s nfsroot strlcat() → snprintf() has Reviewed-by from Justin Stitt and Kees Cook; David Laight sketched a broader rewrite that Cook declined as not a fast path.

Negative dentries: VFS RFC, NFS walks along

NeilBrown’s RFC/RFT v2 (18 patches) still does not shrink the negative population. It stops counting parent refs through every child, and teaches a common d_for_each_positive_child iterator to cond_resched(). The NFS delta is splitting nfs_clear_verifier_directory() so that iterator may drop d_lock. Al Viro objected to the cover-letter claim that d_walk() already schedules under rename_lock. Chuck Lever Reviewed-by’d patch 13 and reclassified it as a tmpfs readdir duplicate-entry fix after same-directory rename.

The leftover merge blocker is fsnotify. On the v1 patch 03 thread, Jan Kara rejected moving fsnotify_recalc_mask() off mark->lock; Amir Goldstein supplied a narrower mark.c split so the children-flag walk never runs under that spinlock. Jan ACKed Amir’s patch and offered to take it immediately. Until that lands, the sleepable NFS verifier walk is not mergeable.

Server work that clients will feel

Chuck Lever’s NFSD updates for v7.3 merged on 20 August. Headline for client development: CB_NOTIFY for NFSv4.1 directory delegations. knfsd used to recall a directory delegation on any change; it now watches through fsnotify and pushes adds/removes/renames with filehandle and attributes so a client that implements directory delegations can keep its dcache. RFC 8881bis NOTIFY4_* flags are in nfs_common. The tag also carries NFSv4 revocation-path UAF fixes, an async COPY stateid rework, and Chris Mason’s localio ref-leak fix in nfs_common.

The CB_RECALL_ANY keep-count series reached v5. NFSD currently sends craa_objects_to_keep = 0, which non-Linux clients treat as “return everything.” The Linux client ignores the field and returns one unused delegation, so Linux-on-Linux testing never saw it. v5 also stops sending CB_RECALL_ANY to NFSv4.0 clients, which permanently marked the callback channel down (RENEW → NFS4ERR_CB_PATH_DOWN). Cedric Blancher asked for a 5.10 backport; Lever declined (applies to ~6.18) and called clients that dump every delegation on a zero keep count defective. A companion trace eval-map patch lets perf/trace-cmd decode the type mask (Hellwig + Layton Reviewed-by).

NeilBrown agreed to drop patch 18/18 of his nfs4_create_file() series after pynfs DELEG8 hung: non-creating OPEN no longer returned NFS4ERR_DELAY during delegation recall because do_lookup_open() has no nfsd_open_break_lease(). Patches 01–17 stay; the shared vfs_lookup_open() work is parked until lease-break can be taught to that path. Linux NFS clients depend on DELAY-and-retry here.

Ameer Hamza’s empty auth.unix.gid replies were applied to nfsd-testing: a failed mountd group lookup was cached as a successful empty list, stripping supplementary groups for 30 minutes. Channel and netlink paths both now install CACHE_NEGATIVE. Client-visible EACCES on --manage-gids project trees after a brief NSS glitch goes away.

Chuck Lever posted a slab overwrite in the NFSv4.1 session reply cache (Jérémy Jean’s KUnit fixture): a legal CREATE_SESSION that negotiates ca_maxresponsesize_cached down to NFSD_MIN_HDR_SEQ_SZ plus a fat cachethis SEQUENCE copies the whole reply past a zero-length slot. The fix preflights SEQUENCE before consuming the slot seqid (RFC 8881 §2.10.6.1.2).

Hellwig NACK’d Hamza’s SUNRPC reply-page recycling premise (don’t special-case copy-splice filesystems as NFSD exports). The split-out fsnotify/atime iterator-READ fixes fared little better: atime is withdrawn (file_accessed() is not NFSD’s job); the duplicate access event is real but Lever kicked it to fsnotify maintainers rather than paper over splice.

syzbot produced a C reproducer for the two-year hung nfsd_umount, a sibling hung write_versions, and the stack-guard hit in nfsctl_transaction_write that Lever’s already-claimed socket reject targets (Layton Reviewed-by, changelog nit on svc_tcp_listen_data_ready). An AI-generated 128-cap listener patch was declined: Layton restated the stall as synchronous rpcbind under nfsd_mutex. On the overlapping nfsd-nl-hang v2 thread, Lever accepted Layton’s policy that rpcbind registration becomes non-fatal for NFSv2/v3; v3 will drop the partial-unwind patch.

Userspace

Steve Dickson tagged nfs-utils-2.9.3-rc1 with three fixes: exportfs --disable-nfsdctl link, getport <stddef.h> for musl, and the statd SM_MON dns_name leak. Petr Vorel posted an unacked getport FTBFS for pre-C23 compilers.

What to watch next week

  • Which of the three overlapping xdr_inline_decode() wrap patches (if any) becomes the vehicle — helper vs. word-at-a-time vs. four-line guard.
  • Pickup of Zuber’s mkdir d_drop, Ran’s lockd unwind, Yoshida’s pipefs_sb, Jean’s kzalloc_objs, and Paula’s slot-table / xdr_inline_pages writes.
  • Trond’s NACK leaves the FlexFiles restart storm unfixed; a layoutreturn that reports the failed layout’s identity rather than silencing the return is still needed.
  • Coddington’s flexfiles notification v2, DIO v6 maintainer ack, and Wilcox’s NFS filemap_invalidate_pages() conversion.
  • NeilBrown absorbing Amir’s fsnotify mark.c split so the sleepable NFS verifier walk can proceed.
  • Layton’s nfsd-nl-hang v3 (non-fatal rpcbind) and Lever’s lockd B_DEAD v2 review.
