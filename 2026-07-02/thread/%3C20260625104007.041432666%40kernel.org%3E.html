tracing v4: Rostedt folds in separate lockdep/instruction_pointer.h fix after Nathan's build break

Summary

tracing v4: the lockdep/_THIS_IP_ build break gets its own patch

Classification: Core-kernel header-cleanup / build-time bug fix (tracing series, v4 → v5 prep). No NFS client behavior or protocol change; the only NFS-tree touch point remains the one-line include addition in include/linux/sunrpc/debug.h from PATCH v4 2/2 (covered in the prior week's summary).

Bridge from last week

Last week, Steven Rostedt posted v4 of his two-patch series splitting trace_controls.h out of trace_printk.h and dropping the latter from kernel.h. Jani Nikula acked the i915 hunks, and Nathan Chancellor closed out the day reporting that patch 2/2 broke lib/test_context-analysis.c with three error: use of undeclared identifier '_THIS_IP_' failures coming through local_lock_internal.h → lockdep.h. He offered a fix diff and asked whether to send it separately or have Rostedt fold it in.

This week's activity (2026-06-26)

Two short messages resolved the open item, both on 2026-06-26.

Rostedt diagnosed the root cause and decided on a separate patch. In his reply, he traced the breakage to a transitive include chain that had been silently carrying instruction_pointer.h (the home of _THIS_IP_) into lockdep:

"Ah, so the reason for this breakage is because lockdep was relying on instruction_pointer.h, that just happened to be included in kernel.h via trace_printk.h."

He then drew the process line clearly:

"This is a separate issue, so it should be a separate patch. I'll add it as patch 1 of this series."

That settles Nathan's earlier question — the fix will land as its own commit ahead of the header split in v5, rather than being folded into patch 2/2 with a note. Rostedt also flagged that he had not seen the failure locally and asked for the reproducing config: "Can you send me the config you used. This didn't trigger in my tests."

Nathan confirmed the diagnosis and supplied two repros. In his follow-up, he first agreed with the root-cause read ("Correct.") and accepted the separate-patch plan ("Sounds good, thanks!"). He then gave Rostedt a plain allmodconfig on arm as the primary reproducer — building just lib/test_context-analysis.o triggers the same _THIS_IP_ error in lockdep.h:541 inside lock_map_acquire(). To make the bug easier to bisect, he also produced a minimal config on top of allnoconfig:

"I also reproduced it on top of allnoconfig:"

CONFIG_CONTEXT_ANALYSIS_TEST=y
CONFIG_DEBUG_KERNEL=y
CONFIG_DEBUG_LOCK_ALLOC=y
CONFIG_EXPERT=y
CONFIG_MMU=y
CONFIG_RUNTIME_TESTING_MENU=y

That minimal set is the actionable deliverable of the week: it explains why Rostedt's own tests missed it (the combination of CONTEXT_ANALYSIS_TEST with lockdep on arm) and gives him a fast, targeted way to verify the new patch 1 before v5 goes out.

Bottom line for NFS readers

  • No NFS client code or protocol changes this week; the SUNRPC impact is still just the explicit #include <linux/trace_printk.h> in include/linux/sunrpc/debug.h from last week's patch 2/2.
  • The outstanding build regression is now scoped and scheduled: a new patch 1 will add the missing instruction_pointer.h include so lockdep no longer depends on it arriving transitively through kernel.h → trace_printk.h. Once that lands, v5 of the series should be unblocked.
  • Watch for Rostedt to post v5 with the new patch 1; Nathan's minimal arm config is the acceptance test.
