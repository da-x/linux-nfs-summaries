Breakage in ktls-utils with NFS Keyring

Overview

This brief thread reports a regression in the ktls-utils userspace tooling when interacting with the NFS keyring for client certificate and private key management. The discussion is classified as a bug fix investigation, targeting an issue introduced by a recent kernel commit related to TLS handling.

Key Technical Arguments

Sagi Grimberg reported that upstream ktls-utils was failing to pass client certificates and private keys using the .nfs keyring. Through bisecting, he identified the culprit:

"Bisecting leads commit facd084e43fc ("tlshd: Client-side dual certificate support")." Sagi Grimberg

Grimberg noted that he had manually applied a workaround patch that restored keyring functionality, though he expressed skepticism about its correctness:

"I manually apply this (probably wrong) change and keyring works... But, I have a feeling its not the correct change..." Sagi Grimberg

The regression stems from changes in tlshd (TLS helper daemon) intended to support dual certificates on the client side, which inadvertently broke compatibility with the existing .nfs keyring mechanism used by ktls-utils.

Evolution of the Discussion

The thread consisted of only two messages on April 30, 2026:

  1. Initial Report: Sagi Grimberg opened the thread by reporting the breakage and providing the bisection result pointing to commit facd084e43fc. He also shared a manual workaround patch (omitted in the summary for brevity) that restored functionality.

  2. Triage Request: Chuck Lever responded quickly, cc'ing the ktls-utils development list and explicitly requesting triage from Scott:

"Scott, can you triage this?" Chuck Lever

Final Conclusions

As of the end of the week, no definitive fix was merged or agreed upon. The thread served as a bug report and triage handoff:

  • The root cause was identified: commit facd084e43fc ("tlshd: Client-side dual certificate support") introduced the regression.
  • A temporary workaround exists but is acknowledged as potentially incorrect.
  • Responsibility for further investigation was delegated to the ktls-utils maintainers, specifically Scott.

This issue highlights ongoing challenges in NFS client TLS infrastructure, particularly around keyring integration and certificate management as the kernel adds more sophisticated dual-certificate support.
