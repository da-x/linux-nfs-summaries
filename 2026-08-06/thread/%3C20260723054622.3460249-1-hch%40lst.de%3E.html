Song acks Hellwig’s swap_ops v2, flags a header-merge nit

Summary

Kairui Song returned to Christoph Hellwig’s swap_ops updates v2 this week and gave the three-patch MM series a full ack. The thread is not an NFS protocol change. It is last month’s swap-ops refactoring plus a targeted swap-writeback fix, and the NFS-relevant piece is still the client-side move off the VFS swap_rw hook onto first-class swap_ops.

Last week’s posting (summarized separately) already had MM acks from Chris Li and Usama Arif. Song is the engineer who originally reported the zram regression that motivated patch 1, so his sign-off is the missing reporter ack, not a new design debate.

What landed this week

Song’s 4 August reply is the only in-window mail. He endorsed the series as-is and singled out the synchronous-device writeback fix:

"This series looks good to me and we should have them, especially patch 1 to ensure existing workloads won't hit a regression"

That is the important bug-fix note for NFS readers even though the patch itself lives in mm/page_io.c. Patch 1 restores per-folio submission for synchronous swap devices (zram) so the classic LRU scan can clear writeback in the scan loop. NFS swap remains asynchronous file-backed I/O and stays on the batched path; the client change is still patch 3, which deletes the old fs/nfs/direct.c swap-rw helper and registers NFS-specific swap_ops from fs/nfs/file.c.

Song also flagged a merge issue, not a functional one:

"minor conflict in patch 2 for unstable or new, due to https://lore.kernel.org/all/20260723032434.2016749-2-ridong.chen@linux.dev/, but should be easy to fix."

Patch 2 is the include/linux/swap_ops.h split that lets implementations live outside mm/page_io.c. The clash is with Ridong Chen’s contemporaneous MM work on unstable/new, not with the NFS client hookup. Song treated it as a mechanical rebase.

He closed with:

"Acked-by: Kairui Song kasong@tencent.com"

NFS client status

Nothing in this week’s mail revisits the NFS or SMB swap_ops tables, the swap_fs_prepare_rw helper, or the removal of VFS swap_rw. Those changes still stand as posted on 23 July: one fewer indirect call on swap-over-NFS, with the client preparing the iov_iter and submitting through the existing NFS direct-I/O path.

No NFS maintainer has commented on the direct.c → file.c relocation in either week. The discussion remains an MM-side ack pile-up around infrastructure that the NFS client will consume.

Where things stand

This is still infrastructure plus a zram writeback fix. After Song’s ack the series has reporter-plus-MM endorsement on all three patches, with only a noted header-file conflict against mm-unstable/mm-new. Hellwig has not posted a v3 in this window, and the NFS client swap-ops hookup is still waiting on filesystem review.
