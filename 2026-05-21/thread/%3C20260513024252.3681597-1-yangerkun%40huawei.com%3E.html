NFSD revert of deferred export cleanup: testing confirmed, queued for nfsd-testing

Summary

NFSD Revert of Deferred Export Cleanup: Testing Confirmed and Queued

Bridge from Prior Week

This thread began last week with Yang Erkun's Patch v2 to revert commit 48db892356d6 ("NFSD: Defer sub-object cleanup in export put callbacks"), which had introduced an unmount regression in the NFS server. Chuck Lever applied the patch to nfsd-testing with an expanded commit message, and Yang Erkun acknowledged the improvement. The technical debate over whether the revert was safe from Use-After-Free (UAF) bugs had been resolved by Jeff Layton's Reviewed-by tag, after Yang demonstrated that sub-objects (dentry, mnt, auth_domain) are themselves freed via RCU mechanisms.

This Week's Activity

This week saw the final validation and integration steps for the revert patch, with two messages on May 18.

Testing Confirmation from Oracle

Calum Mackay reported successful testing of the revert, confirming that all three symptoms associated with the original regression were resolved:

"Testing looks good, Chuck: no crashes, sosreport no longer hangs, no seq_file warnings."

He also provided a Tested-by tag on behalf of Alexandr Alexandrov:

"Tested-by: Alexandr Alexandrov alexandr.alexandrov@oracle.com"

This was significant because the original regression had manifested as crashes during unmount, hangs in sosreport collection, and seq_file warnings — all of which stemmed from the deferred cleanup workqueue mechanism interfering with the export lifecycle. The clean test results validated that reverting to direct sub-object release (relying on RCU protection) was both safe and effective.

Chuck Lever's Acknowledgment and Queueing

Chuck Lever confirmed receipt of the test results and indicated the patch's path forward:

"Thanks. Marked in my private tree, will appear in nfsd-testing on the next push."

This confirmed that the revert (commit ef4e34669aa1) was already applied last week but would be re-pushed with the updated testing metadata. The patch was now fully validated by independent Oracle engineers and ready for upstream inclusion.

Key Technical Points

  • Bug fix type: This is a revert to fix an NFSD unmount regression, not a new feature or protocol change.
  • Root cause of the original bug: Commit 48db892356d6 had introduced deferred cleanup via workqueues for ex_path and ex_client->name in svc_export_put, which caused crashes, hangs, and warnings during unmount operations.
  • Why the revert is safe: Sub-objects are freed via RCU (call_rcu), so direct release in svc_export_put does not introduce UAF risks for RCU readers iterating the export cache.
  • Validation: Independent testing by Oracle confirmed resolution of all three regression symptoms (crashes, sosreport hangs, seq_file warnings).

Final Conclusions

The revert patch is fully validated and queued. Chuck Lever's confirmation that it will "appear in nfsd-testing on the next push" signals the final step before upstream merge. The NFSD export cleanup path returns to direct sub-object release, simplifying the codebase and eliminating the workqueue complexity that caused the regression. This closes out a multi-week discussion that began with the original problematic commit and progressed through v1 and v2 patch iterations to reach a clean, tested resolution.
