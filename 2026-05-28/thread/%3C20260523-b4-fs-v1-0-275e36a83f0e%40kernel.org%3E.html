fs: replace __get_free_pages() with kmalloc() — 17-patch series merged into vfs-7.2.misc

Summary

fs: replace __get_free_pages() calls with kmalloc()

Type: Cleanup / API modernization (not a new feature or protocol change) Status: Merged into the vfs-7.2.misc branch of vfs/vfs.git on 2026-05-27.

Overview

On Saturday, May 23, Mike Rapoport (Microsoft) posted a 17-patch series titled "[PATCH 00/17] fs: replace __get_free_pages() call with kmalloc()" announcing the work. The series is part of a broader kernel-wide effort to retire direct page allocator calls (__get_free_page(), __get_free_pages(), get_zeroed_page()) in favor of the slab allocator APIs (kmalloc(), kzalloc()), which offer better scalability and more debugging hooks.

"This is a (small) part of larger work of replacing page allocator calls with kmalloc." — Mike Rapoport, cover letter

The patches touch 20 files across quota, proc, ocfs2/dlm, nilfs2, NFS, NFSD, libfs, jfs, jbd2, isofs, fuse, fs/select, fs/namespace, configfs, binfmt_misc, and bfs — netting 68 insertions against 89 deletions.

NFS-Relevant Patches

Two patches directly affect the NFS client:

  1. [PATCH 05/17] NFS: replace __get_free_page() with kmalloc() in nfs_show_devname() — swaps a temporary page allocation for kmalloc() in the device-name display path in fs/nfs/super.c.

  2. [PATCH 06/17] NFS: remove unused page and page2 in nfs4_replace_transport() — this is effectively a dead-code / bug-fix cleanup. Rapoport noted that the two temporary page buffers allocated by nfs4_replace_transport() and passed to nfs4_try_replacing_one_location() were "never used." The patch removes them along with all associated allocation and free code, trimming 14 lines from fs/nfs/nfs4namespace.c.

A third patch touches the NFSD server side:

  1. [PATCH 07/17] NFSD: replace __get_free_page() with kmalloc() in nfsd_buffered_readdir() — converts the staging buffer allocation in fs/nfsd/vfs.c. NFS maintainer Jeff Layton acked this quickly, with only a minor nit:

"nit: Don't need double parenthesis here" — Jeff Layton, on the kfree((buf.dirent)) line

He confirmed: "The rest seems ok though. Acked-by: Jeff Layton."

Review and Discussion

The review phase ran from May 23 through May 28, with maintainers across multiple subsystems weighing in:

  • Jan Kara (VFS/quota) was the most active reviewer, picking up patches 01, 02, 03, 10, 11, 13, 14, and 15. He added the quota and isofs patches directly to his tree and provided Reviewed-by tags for the rest. His most substantive technical feedback was on PATCH 14/17 (fs/namespace), where he flagged two issues with the switch to __getname():

    "Fair but d_path() below should then get PATH_MAX and not PAGE_SIZE." "And __putname() is fine with NULL so no need for the if (buf) check here."

    Rapoport agreed on both points, replying "Ack" and "Will fix."

  • David Laight raised an interesting question about PATCH 10/17 (jbd2):

    "Would kvalloc() be more appropriate here? Does __get_free_pages() return physically contiguous memory?"

    Matthew Wilcox answered tersely: "no" (to kvalloc) and "yes" (to physical contiguity). Laight closed the exchange with a simple "Thankyou."

  • Miklos Szeredi acked the fuse patch (PATCH 12/17).

  • Viacheslav Dubeyko and Ryusuke Konishi both acked the nilfs2 patch (PATCH 04/17). Konishi added: "This conversion looks reasonable and won't affect the behavior of the ioctls that use the modified function."

  • Joseph Qi reviewed the ocfs2/dlm patch (PATCH 03/17).

Final Outcome

On May 27, VFS maintainer Christian Brauner applied the entire 17-patch series to the vfs-7.2.misc branch:

"Applied to the vfs-7.2.misc branch of the vfs/vfs.git tree. Patches in the vfs-7.2.misc branch should appear in linux-next soon."

The following day, Brauner confirmed he had folded Jan Kara's suggested fixes for the fs/namespace patch himself: "I've folded this myself."

Significance for NFS

While the series is a broad filesystem cleanup, it is worth noting for NFS developers that PATCH 06/17 removes genuinely dead code in the NFSv4 transport-replacement path (nfs4_replace_transport() / nfs4_try_replacing_one_location()). The two page buffers had been allocated and passed around but never actually dereferenced — a small vestige of earlier design that this series finally excised. Combined with the straightforward kmalloc() swap in nfs_show_devname(), the NFS client's memory allocation paths become marginally cleaner and more consistent with the rest of the kernel.
