Elfring pings rejected nfsd seq_putc() cleanup

Summary

This week produced no NFS client work and no bug-fix discussion. The only in-window traffic is a two-year-old style cleanup ping on the NFSD (server) side, not a protocol change and not a functional fix.

Prior context (July 2024)

On 13 July 2024, Markus Elfring posted [PATCH] nfsd: Use seq_putc() in seq_quote_mem(), a Coccinelle-generated one-liner against fs/nfsd/nfs4state.c. The helper seq_quote_mem() wraps exported state data in double quotes for seq_file output; Elfring wanted the surrounding " characters emitted with seq_putc() instead of seq_puts():

"Double quotation characters should be put into a sequence around other data. Thus use the function “seq_putc” accordingly."

The thread never attracted an on-list review. Patchwork later marked the submission Rejected, with no rejection rationale appearing in this conversation.

This week: a status inquiry, not a respin

On 18 July 2026, Elfring replied to his own patch to ask why the idea was closed:

"Why is the information “State Rejected” presented by a known communication interface for such a development idea?"

He pointed at the linux-nfs Patchwork entry and at a still-present “remaining update candidate” in seq_quote_mem() on Elixir for v7.2-rc3 (fs/nfsd/nfs4state.c around the quote-wrapping seq_puts() calls). There is no new diff, no claimed user-visible bug, and no client-side impact: the function is NFSD seq_file formatting, not the NFS client.

No maintainer answered in-window. The cleanup remains rejected in Patchwork, unexplained on-list, and unmerged.

Takeaway

This is a dormant micro-cleanup of NFSD debug/seq output, revived only as a process question. It does not advance NFS client development and does not document an important bug fix. Until someone from NFSD maintainership states why seq_putc() was declined—or the helper is rewritten for other reasons—the 2024 change is still dead.
