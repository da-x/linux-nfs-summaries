f2fs takes RFC PG_private patch; NFS client still untouched

Summary

f2fs merges RFC slice of PG_private removal; NFS writeback path still waiting

This week’s only traffic on Zi Yan’s original RFC is a patchwork bot notice from patchwork-bot+f2fs@kernel.org. Jaegeuk Kim applied RFC 06/14 (fs/f2fs: stop using PG_private) to jaegeuk/f2fs.git (dev) as 5ad9409a9533. That is a filesystem-specific conversion of f2fs’s PAGE_PRIVATE_NOT_POINTER packing, not an NFS client change, not a protocol change, and not a bug fix.

The bot restates the series goal in the usual mechanical way:

"This patchset removes PG_private to make space for upcoming PG_folio (reserved as __PG_folio) for identifying pages from a folio"

and then:

"Instead of checking PG_private, all code is changed to check page/folio->private != NULL instead."

Where the NFS client still sits

The RFC landed on linux-nfs in the week of 31 July as a core-MM flag-space reclaim. NFS was listed as an exceptional ->private user because writeback request state (nfs_page / page-group) lives on folio->private over a wider refcount window than folio_attach/detach_private(). Yan’s own inventory said of the client:

"6. fs/nfs/write.c: folio refcount maintenance is in a bigger scope than folio->private. So folio_attach/detach/get_private() is not used. Nothing to change."

The only NFS-touching patches in the RFC remain 11/14 (delete the now-no-op folio_set/clear_private() calls in fs/nfs/write.c) and 13/14 (rewrite PagePrivate comments in fs/nfs/file.c). Trond Myklebust and Anna Schumaker are still on the To: line of those patches. Neither has replied on this thread.

Between the prior summary and this window, Yan acknowledged Sashiko nits on comments in include/linux/mm.h, Ceph, and UBIFS — not NFS — and said he would fix them. That mail is outside this week.

What this week does not change for NFS

  • No Acked-by / Reviewed-by from NFS maintainers.
  • No follow-up to convert NFS onto folio_attach/detach_private().
  • No claimed NFS or xfstests coverage.
  • The f2fs merge does not pull the treewide helper deletion that actually edits fs/nfs/write.c.

A v2 of the same series was posted on 31 August as a separate thread (NFS patches 11/14 and 13/14 again), so this RFC thread is effectively being superseded even as f2fs takes the original 06/14. Until someone applies the treewide helper-removal patch, the NFS client still carries the redundant folio_set/clear_private() markers on the writeback path.

Bottom line: this week is a downstream f2fs pickup of one RFC slice. The NFS client impact is unchanged from the August RFC: two deleted no-op flag calls and comment wording, still unreviewed by NFS maintainers.
