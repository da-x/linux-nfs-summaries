Folio-alloc cleanups: Jackman drops the internal-header patch, Babka acks the rest

Summary

Type: API cleanup / header-hygiene series in mm — not an NFS client feature, not a protocol change, and not a bug fix. The only NFS-adjacent footprint remains the mechanical folio_alloc_node() rename in net/sunrpc/svc.c from patch 2/3. This week's two messages close the two-week argument over whether mempolicy-aware folio allocation should be hidden behind a new internal header.

Prior weeks, briefly

On July 16 Brendan Jackman posted a three-patch "yet more cleanups for page_alloc APIs" series on mm-new. Patch 1/3 moved unused-outside-mm mempolicy helpers into a new mm/mempolicy.h; 2/3 replaced __folio_alloc_node() with folio_alloc_node() treewide (including sunrpc); 3/3 pulled __folio_alloc() into mm/page_alloc.h. The "ulterior motive" was threading an mm-internal alloc_flags argument (ALLOC_UNMAPPED for AS_NO_DIRECT_MAP) without inventing another GFP flag.

Matthew Wilcox objected that hiding mempolicy-aware allocation "says allocations which respect mempolicy are only for core mm," and later rejected "no current external users" as a reason to put symbols in internal.h. Gregory Price reviewed 2/3 and 3/3 cleanly but was uneasy about 1/3 because of guest_memfd + mempolicy work. Vlastimil Babka split the difference: hide now and unhide on request, and noted 1/3 was not load-bearing. Last week Jackman offered to skip the hide and ship a public wrapper immediately. No merge decision was recorded.

This week: the controversial patch is dropped

A week later, Jackman came back with a concrete resolution rather than another round of header philosophy. He had already posted v3 of the follow-on page_alloc-unmapped series without depending on this cleanup, introducing the underscored internal helper directly:

"Uh yeah so in v3 I did not depend on this patch, I just went straight to creating __folio_alloc_mpol_noprof() here:

https://lore.kernel.org/all/20260726-page_alloc-unmapped-v3-25-6f5729aa9832@google.com/

So yeah I'd say we can just drop this controversial patch and move forwards with the other two?"

That is the fallback he sketched last week — public wrapper now, internal __ variant only where the extra alloc_flags argument is actually needed — now executed off-list in the unmapped-folio series. Patch 1/3 therefore no longer has a customer. The remaining two patches (the treewide __folio_alloc_node() → folio_alloc_node() rename, including net/sunrpc/svc.c, and the __folio_alloc() move into mm/page_alloc.h) were already reviewed by Price and had no outstanding objections.

Babka's reply is a one-word close:

"Ack."

Where things stand

The design fight over internal.h signalling is over for this series: Jackman will not hide the mempolicy folio helpers, Wilcox's "don't use 'no current callers' as a move-to-internal metric" objection is honored by deletion rather than by a rewrite, and Babka has signed off. Patches 2/3 and 3/3 are the surviving cleanup; 1/3 is abandoned. The leftover bulk-mempolicy helpers Babka spotted in gfp.h last week become moot if 1/3 never lands.

Nobody posted a v2 of this three-patch series in-window, and there is still no explicit merge mail for 2/3 and 3/3. The functional follow-on — ALLOC_UNMAPPED / AS_NO_DIRECT_MAP via __folio_alloc_mpol_noprof() — has already moved to Jackman's separate v3 unmapped series and is no longer blocked on this thread.

For NFS client readers the takeaway is unchanged, only firmer: this is mm header hygiene that happens to rename one sunrpc folio allocation site. No NFS protocol change, no client bug fix, and no new allocator knobs for the network filesystem path. Sunrpc stays on the public folio_alloc_node() side of the split; nobody is proposing that RPC or the NFS client grow alloc_flags or alloc_context awareness.
