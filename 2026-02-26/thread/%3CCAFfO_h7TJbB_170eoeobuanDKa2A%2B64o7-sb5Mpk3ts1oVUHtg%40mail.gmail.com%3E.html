OPENAT2_REGULAR Patch Series: Mailing List Delivery Issues Resolved

Overview

This brief thread addresses a process and tooling issue rather than a technical NFS protocol change or bug fix. Developer Dorjoy Chowdhury encountered difficulties with the visibility of his patch series ([PATCH v4 0/4] OPENAT2_REGULAR flag support in openat2) on the Linux kernel lore archive, prompting assistance from contributor Askar Safin regarding email delivery best practices for large CC lists.

Discussion Evolution

Initial Concern: Missing Patches on Lore Archive

Dorjoy Chowdhury reported that his patch series appeared incomplete on the linux-fsdevel lore archive, with only patches 2/4, 3/4, and 4/4 visible, while the cover letter and patch 1/4 were missing. He noted:

"I am not sure if my patch series made it properly to the mailing lists. https://lore.kernel.org/linux-fsdevel/ is showing a broken series, only the 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and I don't see cover-letter or 1/4."

He used git send-email with a large CC list generated via get_maintainers.pl and wondered if Gmail imposed limitations on sending to many recipients.

Resolution: Temporary Archive Issue

Askar Safin confirmed that all five emails were indeed present in the archive, indicating a temporary indexing or display issue on lore.kernel.org rather than a delivery failure As confirmed by Askar:

"I see all 5 emails on https://lore.kernel.org/linux-fsdevel/CAFfO_h7TJbB_170eoeobuanDKa2A+64o7-sb5Mpk3ts1oVUHtg@mail.gmail.com/T/#t . So this was some temporary problem on lore.kernel.org."

Best Practices for Large CC Lists

Safin provided practical advice for contributors using Gmail to send patches with extensive recipient lists, recommending specific flags to reduce the likelihood of email rejection:

"Sometimes gmail indeed rejects mails if you try to send too many emails to too many people. So I suggest adding '--batch-size=1 --relogin-delay=20' to your send-email invocation."

He also suggested applying for a free linux.dev email address as an alternative for Linux contributors experiencing persistent Gmail issues.

Confirmation

Dorjoy Chowdhury acknowledged the advice and confirmed that the missing patches eventually appeared on the archive As confirmed by Dorjoy:

"Yes, indeed. They showed up after a while."

Conclusion

The discussion concluded with the resolution of a transient archive display issue and the sharing of valuable tooling recommendations for patch submission. No technical changes to NFS client code or protocol behavior resulted from this thread; it served as a helpful exchange on mailing list hygiene and email delivery practices for kernel contributors.
